First release, matching the Android app 1.0.1.

- Basic mode: 1–12 six-sided dice, shown as pips.
- Advanced mode: up to 12 each of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100.
- Results with an optional total. The total stays blank while the dice roll and appears when they stop.
- Optional session history, never saved.
- Light, dark or system theme.
- Custom dice colors.
- Runs entirely in the browser. Settings are saved in the browser only. No ads or tracking.
- Installable, and works offline once loaded. Updates itself when a new version is released.
- Self-host with Docker, or in an Alpine LXC with `scripts/alpine-install.sh`. Both serve on port 8080. For HTTPS, use Tailscale Serve or a reverse proxy.
